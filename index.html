Your perfect stay

A quiet corner of Candolim–Sinquerim

Victoria Villa sits in a quiet and peaceful coastal location in the village of Candolim–Sinquerim. It is only 13 km from the state capital, Panjim, and just a 5 minute walk from a beautiful, relaxing beach with views of the blue waters of the Arabian Sea.

Cleanliness, a homely environment and personalised care are what we offer every guest – so your holiday in Goa is one to cherish.
